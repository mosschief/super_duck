# Design Loads: `open_duck_wheeled_v2_gim6010.xml`

Generated by `tools/export_design_loads.py` (ideal sensors). Loads each joint transmits from parent to child at its anchor: through the motor's output bearing and into the brackets either side. **F_axial / F_radial**: force along / across the joint axis. **M_axis**: moment about the axis (motor torque). **M_bend**: moment across the axis (tilts the bearing; what the bracket and the motor flange bolts carry).

Robot weight 69.0 N. Values are the **99.5th percentile** over the scenario (peak in brackets). For printed parts apply a safety factor of at least 2 on p99.5 (impacts, fatigue, layer adhesion), and check the peak too. Where the M_axis peak exceeds the actuator's own limit, the extra comes from the joint hitting its limit (the hard stop carries it) or from the motor's reflected rotor inertia being stopped suddenly (a shock load through the gearbox, e.g. a spinning wheel touching down).
