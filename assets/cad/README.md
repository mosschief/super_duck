# Sim meshes (generated)

Written by `cad/freecad/export_sim.py` from the FreeCAD assembly (`cad/freecad/super_duck.FCStd`); do not edit.
`<body>__<material>.stl`: every part of a body merged per colour (visual). `<part>_hull.stl`: convex hulls of the
parts that take contacts. Metres, in the MJCF body frames.

The neck, head, antenna, STS3215 and SG90 shapes are derived from the Open Duck Mini v2
(https://github.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Mini, Apache-2.0). The GIM6010-8, GIM4305-10 and Raspberry Pi 5 shapes are
envelope models (`cad/freecad/duck_parts.py`), not the vendor STEP files.
